FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need extra hardware or cables?

No. Both PCs only need to be on the same network, over Ethernet or Wi-Fi. Trovul plays through the headset, speakers and devices you already have.

Which versions of Windows?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Sending only the apps you choose needs Windows 11 on the PC that sends; everything else works on both.

Does my audio go over the internet?

No. The PCs talk directly to each other on your local network, and only PCs you paired are accepted.

How much delay is there?

About 30 ms in Low mode on a wired network and 50 ms by default. On a busy Wi-Fi, Stable mode adds a little delay and removes the dropouts. Trovul shows the live figure and suggests the right mode.

Does it work with games?

Yes. Any app that plays sound shows up in the mixer, games included. Game mode keeps the pointer inside a game until you leave it, and pop-ups never appear over a full-screen game.

Does it replace Mouse Without Borders or Synergy?

For two PCs, yes: keyboard and mouse sharing, the clipboard and files are built in, on the same link as the sound — including admin windows, UAC prompts and the lock screen.
